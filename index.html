Social DM appointment setter

Demo concept · Illustrative description

New enquiries can go cold while someone is waiting for a reply. This concept responds to incoming social messages, collects key details, and guides suitable leads toward an appointment.

  1. Trigger

    A new enquiry arrives in a connected social inbox.

  2. Process

    Reply using approved information, ask qualifying questions, and offer the next booking step. Route uncertain requests to a person.

  3. Output

    A booking request or human handoff, with the conversation recorded in the CRM.

Missed-call text-back

Demo concept · Illustrative description

A missed call can leave a potential customer without a next step. This concept sends a text after a missed call so the caller can explain what they need and continue the conversation.

  1. Trigger

    The connected phone system records a missed call.

  2. Process

    Check messaging permissions and duplicate events, send an approved reply, and route the caller’s response to the right person.

  3. Output

    A follow-up conversation and a contact record for the team.

Inactive lead reactivation

Demo concept · Illustrative description

Older leads can sit in the pipeline without a clear next action. This concept identifies eligible contacts who have gone quiet and starts a relevant follow-up without contacting opted-out or closed records.

  1. Trigger

    A scheduled check finds leads with no recent activity.

  2. Process

    Check status and contact permission, prepare a relevant message, and stop follow-up when a reply or status change occurs.

  3. Output

    Replies routed to the team and CRM records updated with the follow-up result.

Invoice & payment reconciliation

Demo concept · Illustrative description

Comparing invoices with payment records by hand takes time and can leave mismatches unnoticed. This concept matches records using defined rules and separates uncertain cases for review.

  1. Trigger

    New invoice or payment data arrives from a connected source.

  2. Process

    Normalize the records, compare references and amounts, and flag missing or ambiguous matches.

  3. Output

    A reconciliation summary with matched records and an exception list for review.
